# Decisions made while building

Short notes on places where the handoff, the Figma and reality disagreed, and what was done about it.

## Data

- **Met search API.** The handoff points at `/public/collection/v1/search`. That endpoint was retired on 2026-10-01 and now returns a 410 notice. Curation used `/public/collection/v1.1/search` (paginated with `limit`/`offset`). Object lookups (`/v1/objects/{id}`) still work and are what the app uses at runtime.
- **Bot protection.** The Met API sits behind Incapsula and blocks bursts of requests. `scripts/fetch-met.mjs` now throttles to about one request per second, retries with backoff, and keeps the previous `paintings.json` record when an object cannot be fetched during a run.
- **Room statements.** The handoff says to keep the existing Great Hall and European Paintings statements, but the screenshots show different copy for both rooms. The screenshot copy won, since the screens have to match at 390px. 19th-Century and American Wing statements are unchanged.
- **Room names.** The handoff table uses long names ("European Paintings, 1250–1800", "Egyptian Art and the Temple of Dendur"). The screenshots use short names in headers, breadcrumbs and door cards, so `rooms.ts` uses the short names everywhere in the UI. The long names stay in `met_floorplan.json` for map labels. The Figma door copy "The Temple of Dendur" is illustrative; the room is "Egyptian Art" and the Temple is one of its destination galleries.
- **Destination galleries.** The floor plan only has one inner gallery block (822). For paintings whose gallery is inside the chosen room's own block, the destination pin is placed at a deterministic spot inside that block (offset from the center, seeded by the object id) so the last leg of the route is always visible. Paintings in a different block (e.g. Great Hall -> Egyptian Art -> Temple of Dendur, or European Paintings -> 19th Century -> 822) route through that block.
- **Walk minutes.** Route length in meters / 80, rounded, minimum 1, plus 60 m for the Grand Staircase on floor 2 walks so a Wheat Field walk reads "about a 4 min walk" like the Figma.
- **Modern and Contemporary replaced by Greek and Roman.** The handoff lists a Modern room (galleries 900s). The Met's modern wing is closed for rebuilding, and a department-wide search found only four public-domain works from Modern and Contemporary on view anywhere (all in the Lehman wing). A 10-painting, on-view, public-domain pool for that room is impossible today. The eighth room is Greek and Roman Art instead: floor 1, `planKey: greek`, galleries 150s to 160s, right off the Great Hall, with a large Open Access pool. The room id is `greek-and-roman`; adding Modern back later is a matter of a rooms entry plus curated paintings once the wing reopens.
